# Kovanica Legal Payment Policy & EU/Croatia Regulatory Compliance

## 1. Introduction
This Legal Payment Policy ("Policy") governs the use of the Kovanica Protocol ("Network") as a payment system and settlement layer. By participating in the Network, you acknowledge and agree to comply with this Policy and all applicable legal frameworks, specifically those originating from the European Union (EU) and the Republic of Croatia.

## 2. EU Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) Regulation
Any entity operating as a Virtual Asset Service Provider (VASP), Crypto-Asset Service Provider (CASP), exchange, or custodial wallet provider on the Kovanica network within the EU must comply fully with the **MiCA Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2023/1114)**. 
- **Authorization**: CASPs must be authorized by the relevant national competent authority (e.g., HANFA in Croatia).
- **Whitepapers**: Any issuance of new assets or tokens on the Kovanica network requires a MiCA-compliant crypto-asset whitepaper.
- **Market Abuse**: The protocol must not be used for market manipulation, insider dealing, or unlawful disclosure of inside information as prohibited by MiCA.

## 3. Croatian Financial Regulations (HANFA & HNB)
Under the jurisdiction of the Republic of Croatia:
- **HANFA (Hrvatska agencija za nadzor financijskih usluga)**: Entities providing crypto-fiat exchange services or custodial wallet services must register with HANFA in accordance with the Croatian Act on the Prevention of Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing (Zakon o sprječavanju pranja novca i financiranja terorizma).
- **HNB (Hrvatska narodna banka - Croatian National Bank)**: Kovanica virtual assets are NOT electronic money (e-money) or fiat currency. The HNB does not guarantee the value of crypto-assets. Users acknowledge the high risk of volatility and potential loss of capital.

## 4. Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & FATF Travel Rule
In compliance with the **EU AML Directive (AMLD6)** and Croatian AML laws:
- VASPs processing Kovanica transactions must implement rigorous Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) procedures.
- **Travel Rule**: Transactions between custodial entities must include originator and beneficiary data in compliance with the EU Transfer of Funds Regulation (TFR).

## 5. GDPR & Data Privacy
The Kovanica ledger is a public, immutable DAG. By using the network, users acknowledge that:
- Transaction data, including public addresses and balances, are permanently visible on the public ledger.
- Personal data should NEVER be stored directly in transaction payloads.
- VASPs interfacing with Kovanica must process off-chain user data in strict compliance with the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**.

## 6. Hardware and Custody Requirements
Institutions processing payments must utilize secure custody solutions. Kovanica requires institutional VASPs to implement Hardware Wallet integrations (e.g., Ledger, Trezor) combined with M-of-N Multisignature setups (RFC-001) to distribute custodial risk and prevent unilateral fund misappropriation, in line with MiCA custody safeguarding requirements.

## 7. Prohibited Uses
The Network may not be used to facilitate illegal commerce, terrorism financing, money laundering, or evasion of international sanctions (including EU restrictive measures). VASPs must actively monitor and report suspicious activity to the Croatian Anti-Money Laundering Office (Ured za sprječavanje pranja novca).
